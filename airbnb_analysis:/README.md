# Airbnb Analysis Findings

# 1) In the Twin Cities, superhosts charge $1.38 less per night than other hosts for entire home.In Chicago and Columbus they charge around $44 and $41 more. So with the badge a twin cities host should not expect higher pricing.
# 2) The top 10% of listings earn 41% of estimated revenue in Chicago, 40 percent in the Twin Cities and 36 percent in Columbus.
# 3) Twin Cities reviews peaked in August 2025 at 2.4 times the February 2026 low; 7,399 vs 3,119. Hosts should expect summer demand to be more than double winter demand.
# 4) Only 19% of Columbus entire homes are unavailable for more than half of October 2026, compared with 22 percent in Chicago.
# 5) North Linden is the cheapest but well-reviewed Columbus neighborhood for a family of four. It sits at a median of $32.55 per person per night. It is only about 20 listings so it is a small sample size.
